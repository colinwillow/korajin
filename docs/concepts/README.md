## Details that show in every view (build these)
- The UNDERSIDE: the island is a cluster of downward stone SPIKES (inverted pyramids), not a rounded rock, with a central jade-core PYLON
  (stacked gold rings, a glowing jade cylinder, a spiked gold tip) hanging from the middle, and smaller ones under the corners.
- HANGING LANTERNS everywhere: gold-capped cages with jade glass and a spiked tip, on chains -- under the island, under every bridge and
  satellite, in clusters. They are the silhouette as much as the temple is.
- SATELLITE PILLARS: inverted-spire towers hanging off the bridge ends at two levels (upper and lower), each with lanterns, a jade core, a
  small pagoda-ish top with a palm; joined to the temple by bridges with RED rope rails on gold posts.
- Warm sandstone blocks with GOLD rivets / studs, jade light slits, gold trim bands, vines and palms on every terrace.
- Distant floating islands (smaller temples) with WATERFALLS pouring off them, a sea of clouds below, clear blue sky.
