# JINRA POWER -- the green as the thing you collect and SPEND (design doc; his, Nov 2 2026)

> *"We definitely need to start using it in game. It's almost like Sonic's rings: it can lead you where you need to go. You'll always be
> collecting it -- to turn the enemies back into regular people, and for portals, moving bridges, floating platforms you need to cross. Put your
> hand out, the hand emits the Jinra, it goes into the thing and the thing starts floating, spinning, moving."*

**What exists:** `JINRA.n` (saved, `mel.jinra`) counts the wisps a dead foe sheds; the colour is `JIN` (mint, the Jinra's alone); gold DOUBLOONS
are money (`COIN`), never green -- so the two stay: **gold buys, Jinra powers.** The garden's Jinra column lifts him (being reworked).

## The loop
**COLLECT** -> **CARRY** (a meter) -> **SPEND** on one of four verbs.
- **Collect:** wisps from foes (exists); TRAILS of small shards laid along routes (the Sonic-ring line: they show the way and the jump); big
  CLUSTERS in hard-to-reach places (a high ledge, behind a breakable wall); springs and crystals that refill slowly.
- **Carry:** a mint meter by the build chip, with a cap that grows over the game (shrines raise it).
- **Spend -- the GIVE gesture:** hold out a hand at a Jinra-dead thing in reach; a stream flows from the palm into it while held; the meter drains;
  when it's full the thing wakes. One verb, many targets:
  1. **LIFT** -- a fallen stone platform rises and hovers (a route across a gap).
  2. **TURN** -- a wheel / a bridge segment / a gate spins into place.
  3. **OPEN** -- a portal or a sealed door lights and opens.
  4. **RESTORE** -- a knocked-down Hollowed: the shell bursts, the person stands up (KORAJIN.md RESTORE).
- **Also spent by:** the sword's JINRA DRAW (raise it among crystals, a charged spinning strike) -- the boss's only answer.
- **The Jinra column / pad:** a LIFTER (stand in it, you rise; in the air you get the air pose; hands-off you settle into meditation; free to
  move and drop out) and, later, LAUNCHERS (a burst column that throws you up).
