## PROMPT 1 -- THE WORLD BIBLE (the cosmology)

I'm making a mobile 3D action-adventure game called KORAJIN (open world, regions linked by land and water channels, temples,
bosses). Help me write the WORLD BIBLE: how the world works, so every region and cut-scene fits one story.

What exists (keep it):
- Hero: KORAJIN GRUMBO, a big, lovable, dozy alien guardian (purple-spotted, orange crest, bronze armour, red cloth). Stubborn,
  mischievous, kind, enjoys a fight a little too much. NOT a chosen one, no amnesia. Tone: warm, funny, a little melancholy;
  strange merchants, grumpy villagers, absurd creatures. Touchstone: Usagi Yojimbo. Japanese-inspired alien culture with
  retro-futuristic bronze tech so old it became tradition.
- JINRA: a luminous, semi-living energy (liquid, vapour or crystal) that flows through everything and makes things float.
  It may come in several COLOURS, each a different power/element (one per region/temple), held in BALANCE.
- CRYSTALS: Jinra crystallises into great power crystals. SKY TEMPLES form a grid that beams energy between them -- a shield
  that keeps the world stable and things afloat. Crystals break into SHARDS; a shard opens a temple door.
- THE BELLS: great bells in ancient sanctuaries whose resonance is tied to the crystals and the floating.
- THE HOLLOW: a swirling purple-black void that drinks Jinra (drained things stop floating and fall; people become stone
  statues, objects become hollow constructs). Behind it: VORAKAI, THE HOLLOW EMPEROR, Devourer of Jinra (unseen early).
- THE RESTORATION: the game is about taking the crystals back and restoring the world. Two moments per region: (1) after a
  temple's boss, the crystal is charged from the temple's torches (THE GATHERING) and the REGION rejuvenates (fallen, dusty,
  eerie red -> plants regrow, colour returns, clear water); (2) returning the crystal to its sky temple RESTORES the fallen
  cities (they rise and float again).
- The day/dusk/night cycle runs fast -- maybe lore: the world's instability, time slipping as stability drains.
- Regions (draft): a giant FOREST (you feel small), SNOW, WATER (the lake, a sea colossus), SAND (a desert pyramid temple with
  floating boulders), FIRE/STONE (Akatani, the red valley -- Grumbo's homeland, the Ember Canyon), maybe LIGHTNING, and AIR
  last (the floating realm; the dragon; the glider). Then the final region.
- Prologue (done): a floating zen garden; the Bell Festival; Grumbo sleeps through his duty; the Hollow strikes, the bell
  cracks, the floating world falls and he lands on the ruined ground below.

The open question I need solved: DO ALL THE REGIONS FLOAT? My lean: only ONE realm floats -- the Korajin's sky realm -- and
it holds the whole world together (all the powers meet there); that's the one that falls in the prologue. The water region is
in the water, etc.

Give me:
1. Three different COSMOLOGIES (half a page each) answering: what floats and why, how the regions and colours relate, what the
   crystals/bells/sky temples really are, why the Hollow wants Jinra, and why the day cycle is unstable. Then pick one and say why.
2. For the chosen one: each region's ELEMENT/COLOUR, its power (what Grumbo gains there), its people and their quirk, what
   the Hollow did to it, its temple, and its one unique landmark.
3. A one-paragraph history of the world before the game.
Constraints: original (no Zelda: Skyward Sword / BotW copies, no chosen-one), show-don't-tell, keep the humour.
