# KORAJIN ENEMY ROSTER -- brainstorm for concept art (Nov 5 2026, open)

For Colin to generate images from, then Tripo -> rig -> game. Lore: the HOLLOW drains Jinra; drained things become stone or
hollow CONSTRUCTS animated by STOLEN Jinra (mint glow at the chest core, eyes and joints). Enemy look = ENEMY_STYLE.md:
black lacquer + aged gold, pale sandstone, terracotta, iron, ONE glow colour (stolen Jinra mint). Every enemy has a STONE TWIN
(STATUES.md). Every enemy needs one readable TELL before it attacks and one WEAK POINT.

## STYLE PREFIX (paste first, every time)
Stylized 3D game character concept, full body, front and side turnaround on a plain background, chunky readable painterly
style, bold silhouette, big simple shapes. An ancient construct made of carved pale sandstone, fired terracotta, black
lacquer and aged gold trim, held together by glowing mint-green energy that shines from a core in the chest, the eyes and the
cracks at the joints. Feudal-Japanese and ancient-alien design, weathered, severe, angular. No red cloth, no realistic or
photographic rendering, no sci-fi chrome.

## THE CORE SQUAD (every region)
1. HUSK SOLDIER -- basic melee. Terracotta warrior with a black-lacquer spear and a small gold-crested helm, chest core
   visible through a cracked breastplate. Tell: spear drawn back, core flares. Weak: the core.
2. HUSK ARCHER -- ranged, takes the high ground. Slimmer terracotta archer, tall black bow, mint arrows, a quiver of glowing
   shafts. Tell: the bowstring glows. Weak: close range.
3. BULWARK -- shield tank. Heavy squat construct behind a huge black-and-gold tower shield with the enemy emblem; you whip the
   shield away or flank it. Tell: shield slam wind-up. Weak: its back.
4. SPHINX HOUND -- fast pack beast. Four-legged lion-bodied stone beast with a blank gold mask for a face, runs in threes,
   pounces. Tell: crouch and mask glow. Weak: the legs (breakable).
5. HOLLOW MOTH -- flying pest that DRAINS. A paper-lantern moth construct, wings of cracked lacquer panels, a mint lantern
   belly; latches on and sips your Jinra. Tell: hovers still, then dives. Weak: the lantern.
6. HOLLOW MONK -- caster / support. Robed stone monk with a gold mask and a staff with a hanging censer; its smoke turns
   things to STONE (slows you, petrifies allies into statues it can later wake). Tell: the censer swings. Weak: break the censer.

## REGION ENEMIES
7. FOREST -- THORNBACK (mid-boss, planned): a burrowing beast of bark-plated stone, a ridge of thorn spikes down its back,
   mint sap in the cracks. Burrows and erupts. Weak: the belly when it rears.
8. FOREST -- ROOTBOUND SENTRY: a temple guardian statue half-swallowed by tree roots, roots for arms that whip and grab.
9. DESERT -- SAND SCORPIONS (placeholders in game; little + giant): segmented sandstone scorpions, gold-banded tail and a
   glowing stinger; the giant has armour plates and a tail-joint weak spot.
10. DESERT -- DUNE RAY: a flat stone manta that swims UNDER the sand with only a gold fin showing, then leaps out.
11. WATER -- REEF CRAB WARDEN: a huge stone crab with a shrine roof for a shell, barnacles, one oversized claw.
12. WATER -- LAMPREY EELS: long segmented terracotta eels with ring mouths, glowing, in packs, underwater.
13. SNOW -- FROST ONI: a hulking ice-and-stone ogre construct, a huge club of frozen iron, frost breath.
14. SKY -- KITE GLIDERS: lacquer-paper kite constructs with spear tails that dive in formation.
