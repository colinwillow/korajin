# THE SKY TEMPLE -- THE ROUTES (every path, planned before it is built)

His (Oct 31, m1923): *"There's a super high ladder you just climb and climb -- that defeats the whole purpose of this game. He can run up walls,
jump between two walls back and forth, wall run; we designed this whole kit. Instead of a fun obstacle course to the top you put a ladder from
bottom to top. Do a million more passes until this temple is flawless and so fun to play. Plan out all of the paths."*

**No ladders on any route. Every gap is sized from the MOVE TABLE (LEVELGRID.md, `tools/moves.json`), never eyeballed.** The temple is built at
x2.5, so the route is written in METRES (`m: 1` entries: `templeScale` does not touch them) -- the architecture grows, his moves do not.

## The numbers it is built from (comfortable = 80% of the measured max -- what a level may ask for)
| move | comfy | used for |
|---|---|---|
| step | .30 | stairs (.3 risers) |
| jump / double jump | 2.18 / 4.01 up | hops onto blocks |
| ledge from a ledge (double jump, .9 m back) | 4.69 up | the pinnacle hops |
| run jump / long jump / double + kicks | 5.95 / 10.98 / 13.6+ across | gaps between pieces |
| wall run UP: run / sprint / run-jump / long-jump | lip 2.65 / 3.61 / 4.16 / 8.52 over his feet | ledges on faces |
| WALL CHAIN (chimney, faces 4.6 m apart) | +1.66 per kick (2.07 max) | the chimneys |
| side wall run / + kick | 5.5-6.5 along / 6 across, +2 up | traverses |
| HIGH BAR launch: for height / for distance | 11.9 up / 19 across (in between at other release angles) | the bars |
| safe fall | 24 m | every fall inside the temple lands somewhere he can climb back from |

## THE GOLDEN PATH (the clearest way up; every stage one mechanic, a REST and a CHECKPOINT after it)
Kishotenketsu over the whole climb: INTRODUCE the chimney low, introduce the BAR, DEVELOP the chimney long, CONCLUDE with ledge hops.
1. **The stair** (plaza 0 -> landing 15). Walk. Checkpoint: the landing.
2. **CHIMNEY I** (15 -> 40): two ancient-tech fins 4.6 m apart on the landing's left, in front of the left pylon, chevrons at their foot. Run-jump in,
   wall-jump back and forth ~13 kicks to the fin tops, a stone bridge onto tier 1. Checkpoint: tier 1, front left. A fall lands on the landing.
3. **THE BARS** (40 -> 50 -> 60): a high bar on tier 1's front-left; launch for height (+10 m, 6 m across) onto a FLOATING PLATFORM; a second bar on it;
   launch for distance (+10 m, 13 m across) onto tier 2's west side. Checkpoint there. A miss drops to tier 1 (10-20 m, safe).
4. **CHIMNEY II** (60 -> 82 -> 105): a fin in two legs beside the tower's west face (stepped to follow its batter: the gap stays 4.6-5.4 m); a REST
   LEDGE at 82 on the tower face halfway (checkpoint); out over the tower's lip at 105. A fall from the upper leg lands on the rest ledge or tier 2.
5. **THE PINNACLE** (105 -> 109.5 -> 113.75): two double-jump ledge hops (4.5 and 4.25 m: inside the 4.69 comfy). The ORB, the halo overhead.
   Checkpoint: the summit.
6. **Down**: the zipline off the tower's front edge to the plaza (the loop back).

## LATER PASSES (planned; each its own build)
- **The watchtowers** (the seals): no ledge ladders -- each tower gets ONE signature move: FR a chimney fin, FL a high bar on a plinth to a mid-tower
  shelf and a wall-run-up, BL/BR side-wall-run + kick ledges round the corner. Their ledge columns (9.75 m apart, beyond any comfy number) go.
- **The upper level** (balconies 60, satellites 75): reached by the pylon-2 ring (75) dropping to the balcony, or a SIDE-WALL-RUN traverse along the pylon
  faces from tier 1; the hatch ladders are gone.
- **Optional branches** (harder, they pay: a seal, a cache, a shortcut): MONKEY BARS from the tower top over to the pylon-3 roof (the halo's chains
  land there); a swing rope off a satellite; long-jump + kick gaps between the floating stones.
- **Shortcuts back down** (loop back): zips from the pylon-3 roof and the satellites.
- **Enemies by zone** (LEVELDESIGN layer 3): never on a rest pad, never firing at a chimney you cannot leave; archers on the bar platform's far side.
