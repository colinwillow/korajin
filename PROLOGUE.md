# PROLOGUE -- the sky falls (draft 1, for his notes; Nov 1 2026)

> *"I'm building a lot of pieces; what really needs to be done is a progression. Start with chapter one and decide how it plays through, instead of
> making the entire game at once. A prologue: an ethereal, floating, green, lush, sun-rayed, Buddhist sky with temples -- kind of like Zelda. It's
> nice in the beginning, then something happens -- a thing crashes, the floating Zen garden comes down to the ground, and you ride it down or
> escape (jump off, a wingsuit, a dragon), or maybe it's a level: jumping rock to rock down to the ground. On the ground: a fallen, decayed, dark
> red-sky haze, people turned to stone statues, a village of disheveled, scared people -- 'the giant pillar stone came down.' Set the theme and pace
> of the whole game. Refine one thing at a time until it's cool, beautiful and fun, then move on -- never more than the step ahead."*

## Why this fits what's already written (KORAJIN.md)
The game's arc is THE RESTORATION: the floating sky world FELL and you lift it back up. A prologue that SHOWS the sky world whole and then SHOWS it
fall is that arc's opening chord -- the player has seen what was lost before they start rebuilding it. Every restoration later is this fall played
backwards (the same `RESTORE` kit, run in reverse here).

## The beats (one line each; each beat is ONE thing to get right)
1. **THE GARDEN (calm, ~1-2 min).** Morning in the floating Zen garden: islands, a temple, sun rays, mint Jinra in the streams, monks/villagers,
   a bell. Grumbo wakes / arrives. The player learns to MOVE and JUMP here, with nothing at stake. Small, beautiful, dense -- one courtyard's worth.
2. **THE OMEN.** Something is wrong: the light dims, the Jinra in the streams drains toward one point, a deep hum. Everyone looks up.
3. **THE STRIKE.** The thing arrives -- a giant falling STONE PILLAR (the villagers' "pillar stone" later), or a Kūkyo construct ripping the Jinra
   out. It hits; the islands crack; the floating stops.
4. **THE FALL (the first real level).** The garden breaks apart and drops. You run and jump DOWN through falling pieces -- rock to rock, temple roof
   to roof -- the camera wide, the sky turning red. Short, intense, hard to fail badly (a miss lands on a lower piece, never a death pit).
5. **THE ESCAPE.** The last piece is going to hit: one big leap off, and a brief glide / ride (a wingsuit-like glide here only, taken away at the
   bottom; or caught by the dragon) to the ground. A cinematic beat.
6. **THE FALLEN GROUND.** Silence, red haze, dust settling on the wreck. People turned to STONE where they stood (THE STOLEN ONES). A frightened
   village nearby -- "the pillar came down" -- and the first person who talks to you. The title card. Chapter One begins.
